## Why Selected

Kieran Flanagan is a SaaS marketing leader known for his deep insights into growth marketing, product-led growth, and modern B2B marketing systems. His LinkedIn content focuses on how marketing is evolving and how companies should rethink acquisition strategies.

## Profile

- SVP of Marketing (SaaS leadership background)
- Strong focus on PLG (Product-Led Growth)
- Writes about modern marketing systems and strategy shifts
- Combines strategic thinking with real-world SaaS experience

## Key Observations

- Thought-leadership style writing (strategic, reflective tone)
- Focus on industry shifts rather than tactical tips
- Often challenges traditional marketing definitions
- Uses opinion-driven hooks to trigger discussion
- High-level conceptual thinking over execution details
