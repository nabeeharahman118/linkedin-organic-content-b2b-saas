# Nicolas Cole

## Why Selected

Nicolas Cole is one of the most influential writers and content strategists on LinkedIn, known for teaching high-level writing systems, audience building, and content monetization. His work focuses on turning writing into a scalable skill for business growth.

## Profile

- Writer, entrepreneur, and content strategist
- Co-founder of Ship 30 for 30
- Focus on writing systems and digital audience building
- Known for teaching structured content creation frameworks

## Key Observations

- Strong emphasis on writing systems over inspiration
- Uses step-by-step frameworks in content
- Focus on simplifying complex writing processes
- Highly educational, tutorial-style posts
- Consistent focus on repeatable writing routines
